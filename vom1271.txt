DESCRIPTION                                                                   • Data acquisition
The VOM1271 is a stand-alone optically isolated MOSFET                        • ATE
driver. Unlike conventional MOSFET drivers, which require
an external power supply to provide VCC and or VDD rails to                   • Isolated solenoid drivers
the driver itself, the VOM1271 obtains all the required                       • Isolated high current relay drivers
current to drive its internal circuitry from the LED current on               • Isolated high voltage relay drivers
the low voltage primary side of the isolation barrier. This
saves the designer the space and cost associated with                         AGENCY APPROVALS
providing one or more external power supplies. The
                                                                              • UL
VOM1271 also integrates a turn-off circuit internal to the
component itself, thus doing away with the need for                           • cUL
additional components in order to increase the overall                        • VDE
switching speed by decreasing the turn-off time. These                        • FIMKO
features, combined with a small SOP4 package, provide
designers with a small footprint, highly integrated isolated
gate driver solution for a large variety of MOSFET driver
applications.
